# History

The tournaments this skill is distilled from.

| Tournament | Shape | What it taught |
|---|---|---|
| Chart design system, Oct 10 (became Marginalia) | 4 directions plus baseline; then 5 rounds of refine, push and challenge around a carried champion; 3 lens judges; 100-point rubric with anchors; audit gate; code tally; 38 agents in about 35 minutes | Fixed fixture plus one build command works. Champions lost 4 to 13 points when rejudged, so rounds 3 to 6 were refinement inside the noise. Every entry converged on one typeface by round 3. Dark mode and non-chart visuals were asked for afterwards. |
| Brand remix, Oct 10 | 7 variants, 3 lens judges, 5 finalists relabeled and rejudged | Identical pixels scored 82.0 then 75.7: gaps under about 6 points are ties. The label key sat in the judges' folder. |
| Skill redesign, Oct 10 | 3 angle proposals, 3 lens judges naming best ideas and rejections, a synthesizer grafting the best ideas | Grafting named best ideas from losers works. A model-computed tally and names inside the judged text weaken it. Design has no failing test; gates are only for what a script can decide. |
| Skill name, Oct 3 | 3 generators, 1 judge | The owner: "the best it could do was pick a piece of my one example." Two angles were built from his example. Never anchor directions on one illustration. |
| Logo, Oct 15 | 6 concepts across the owner's two named worlds; knockout 6 to 4 to 2 to 1; blind concept sheets; small-size renders; clamped code tally with tie-breaks | The first run had no checkpoint after round 1 and was paused there so the owner could keep or cut directions. |
