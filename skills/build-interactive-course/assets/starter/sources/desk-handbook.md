# Desk handbook

A made-up source for the sample course. Every evidence line in the sample points at a line range in this file, so `scripts/check_evidence.py` has something real to check.

## 1. Checking out

### 1.1 Accounts and blocks

A library card is valid for three years. Renew an expired card at the desk with proof of a current address.

A patron without their card is looked up by photo ID. A forgotten card does not block a checkout.

Three things block a checkout:

- an expired card
- fines owed of $10.00 or more
- any item marked lost that has not been settled

Fines under $10.00 do not block a checkout.

Every checkout follows the same routine:

1. Find the account by scanning the card, or by photo ID.
2. Read any blocks on the account.
3. Scan each item.
4. Hand over a receipt with each due date.

### 1.2 Loan periods and limits

| Item       | Loan    | Renewable  |
|------------|---------|------------|
| Book       | 21 days | yes, twice |
| Quick pick | 14 days | no         |
| DVD        | 7 days  | yes, twice |

When a due date falls on a day the library is closed, it moves to the next open day. The receipt shows the moved date.

A patron may have 25 items out at once, and no more than 5 of them DVDs.

## 2. Returns and renewals

### 2.1 Checking in

Scanning an item at check-in ends the loan. The screen then says where the item goes next.

An item with a hold on it goes to the hold shelf with a slip for the patron who placed the hold, and that patron gets a pickup notice.

Every item gets a quick look for damage before it goes on a cart.

### 2.2 Emptying the book drop

Items left in the book drop while the library is closed are checked in as of the last open day. Empty the drop in this order:

1. Set the check-in date to the last open day.
2. Scan every book drop item.
3. Route held items to the hold shelf.
4. Set the date back to today.
5. Open the desk.

Forgetting step 4 checks in every desk return on the wrong day.

### 2.3 Damage

The desk does not charge for damage. Note the damage on the item record and put the item in the repair bin. The branch manager decides whether the damage goes beyond normal wear and whether the patron pays anything.

### 2.4 Renewals

A renewal gives an item a full new loan period, counted from the day of the renewal. Patrons can renew at the desk, by phone or online.

An item cannot be renewed when:

- another patron has a hold on it
- it has been renewed twice
- it is a quick pick

The hold rule is checked when the renewal is made. A renewal stops further fines on the item. Fines already owed stay on the account. Being overdue does not stop a renewal.

## 3. Holds and fines

### 3.1 Holds

Holds are filled in the order patrons place them. A patron can have 15 active holds.

When a held item is checked in, it goes to the hold shelf and the patron gets a pickup notice. The notice starts a 7-day pickup window. An item on the hold shelf goes out only to the patron it is held for.

When the window ends unused, the hold expires and the item moves to the next patron in the queue, who gets a pickup notice of their own. Nobody is charged for letting a hold expire.

### 3.2 Fines

Fines count only the days the library is open, and they show on the account as they build up.

| Item       | Per open day | Cap    |
|------------|--------------|--------|
| Book       | $0.25        | $5.00  |
| Quick pick | $0.25        | $5.00  |
| DVD        | $1.00        | $10.00 |

Returning an item stops new fines. It does not remove fines already owed.

### 3.3 Lost items

At 30 days overdue an item is marked lost and the patron is billed its replacement cost. The replacement charge is kept apart from fines.

A lost item returned within 6 months has its replacement charge removed. The overdue fine stays.
