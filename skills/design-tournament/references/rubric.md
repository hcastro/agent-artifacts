# Rubric

Score on a 100-point scale so totals compare across tournaments. Pick 5 to 7 criteria, write anchors for each, and keep gates separate: a gate disqualifies, a criterion ranks.

## Default criteria

| Criterion | Points | Earns | Loses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sophistication and restraint | 20 | One bold idea, everything else quiet; nothing decorative without a job | Effects stacked to rescue a weak form; every element shouting |
| Brief and taste fit | 20 | Reads as the owner's words and references; the right world (era, mood) on purpose | Generic for the category; a different mood than asked; pastiche |
| Distinctiveness and ownable signature | 15 | One detail nobody else in the category has; clear of competitor and platform marks | Could be any brand in the category; echoes a known mark |
| Craft | 15 | Type set with intent (scale, spacing, weights); geometry, junctions, optical balance resolved | Default weights and spacing; lumpy curves; inconsistent strokes or radii |
| Function and legibility | 15 | Works at the smallest real size and at 375 px; contrast holds; information reads first | Breaks at 16 px or on a phone; decoration competes with content |
| System coherence | 15 | Tokens, components and states feel like one family across surfaces and themes | Each surface invents its own rules; dark mode is an afterthought |

Swap criteria for the artifact: a reference brand gets "brand distance" or "brand fidelity"; data viz splits function into chart legibility and annotation; a logo weights small-size strength and craft higher.

## Calibration (state it to every judge)

Half marks are mediocre. 80% is very good. 95% and up is exceptional and rare. Use the whole range; a judge who scores everything between 70 and 80% gives the tally nothing to work with.

## Gates (pass or fail, scripted)

- Text contrast at least 4.5:1, large text and key marks at least 3:1.
- Text at least 12 px; no transformed or outlined-only body text.
- No horizontal overflow at 375 px; headline at most two lines at 375 px when there is one.
- Fonts are open-licensed or already licensed for the use; marks contain no live text in the final round.
- Palette or brand checks when the brief pins one.
- Artifact-specific audits (for marks: the logo-design skill's `svg_audit.py`).

## Anti-generic list (give to every designer and judge)

Avoid unless the brief asks for it by name: Inter and Space Grotesk as the identity face, Playfair Display, a cream background with a terracotta serif, a near-black page with one acid-bright accent, purple gradients, gradient text, emoji, glow used as decoration, rounded cards with soft shadows everywhere, ALL-CAPS eyebrow labels above every heading, middle-dot meta strings, monospace for small labels by default, arrows appended to links. Category clichés for the artifact go here too (for a streaming mark: play triangles in rounded screens, glitch-cut speech bubbles, broadcast arcs, RGB split).
