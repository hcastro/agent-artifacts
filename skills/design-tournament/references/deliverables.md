# Deliverables

## Gallery (the main deliverable)

A page (an Artifact when available) with every entry from every round: its images (enlargeable), per-criterion scores per judge, total, the strength, weakness and change notes, the round it fell out, and the rubric. A score-by-round chart for carried entries. Verify the gallery's claims against the workflow journal before publishing (every judge returned every round; scores match).

## Owner checkpoints

- **After round 1:** the gallery of directions plus the tally. Ask which to keep or cut. His taste overrides the ranking; record his reasons for the next briefs.
- **Final:** finalists with a recommendation and one honest risk each. He picks.

## Apply the winner

Apply it to the real target, then to one surface it was not tuned on (another page, the dark theme, a small size) and check the gates there. A winner that only works on its fixture isn't done.

## Spec

Tokens with computed contrast pairs, type scale and families, the signature detail and why it is ownable, how it differs from the reference or competitor, and the exact changes to the codebase or skill it lands in.

## Honesty section (always)

- The noise level (rejudged scores, ties declared under the threshold).
- How the field narrowed (shared typefaces or palettes among survivors).
- That the judges were models, and whether a cross-model judge agreed.
- Surfaces and states never rendered.
